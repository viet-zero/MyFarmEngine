# P2-002C — Money, seed selection and atomic trade

Status: DONE kỹ thuật — 2026-11-05.

- Dependencies: P2-002A typed bag, P2-002B crops/home warehouse, stable ItemIds and ownership rule.
- Goal: purchase seeds for all three defined crops and sell produce from the eligible bag/home storage, closing the minimum farming economy loop.
- Acceptance: explicit integer money/prices; unknown IDs, zero/negative/overflow amounts, no funds/stock/capacity fail without partial item/money changes. A trade commits exactly once. A home sale can use owned warehouse without first moving produce into the backpack; outside home/foreign owner cannot spend that warehouse. Seed selector stays compact, no persistent grid on gameplay screen.
- Storage management: if transfer/withdraw is exposed, validate owner/location and capacity on both containers and commit all-or-nothing. Do not silently auto-transfer or consume seeds from warehouse until behavior is explicit.
- Tests: buy→plant/water/harvest→home store→sell→buy loop, retries, full bag/warehouse, insufficient funds/produce, ownership/boundaries and regression Debug/Release + graphics smoke.
- Out of scope: full shop NPC schedule, many currencies, marketplace, new artwork and full roadmap. Persistence follows with SQL Server local pilot and snapshot/rollback/restart tests; no claim current RAM warehouse is durable.


M mở menu, 1–4 chọn hàng, F mua, C bán túi, K bán kho trong vườn, Space chọn hạt có sẵn. Prototype cấp 20 tiền và mỗi lệnh mua/bán một đơn vị. Kiểm thử vòng kinh tế và OpenGL được ghi ở [tiến độ](../PROGRESS.md).
